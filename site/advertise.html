Featured Ranches (homepage)

The homepage has a Featured Ranches section just above the directory, with up to three ranches. A feature shows the owner's photos and their story in their own words, credited to them. We never feature a ranch without the owner's permission, and we only use photos the owner took or holds the rights to.
