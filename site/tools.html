Tax & Money

Not tax, legal or investment advice. These are educational templates. Consult a CPA and your county appraisal district. Each file has an instructions sheet and cites its IRS or Texas Comptroller sources. No rates are pre-filled except published IRS table and statutory figures (cited). Check every file against the current publications, because tax law changes.

Breeding stock depreciation schedule

Purchased breeding animals and improvements: cost, §179 and bonus amounts you enter, then the IRS MACRS 200% DB half-year table (Pub 946 Table A-1). Shows a 10-year schedule plus adjusted basis and recapture if sold.

Sources: IRS Pub 946, Pub 225, Pub 544, Form 4797

Texas ag / wildlife valuation self-check

Yes/No checklist for the 1-d-1 exotic-livestock path and the wildlife-management path (3 of 7 practices, written plan). Exotic animals do not count toward wildlife management valuation.

Sources: Texas Comptroller 96-300, 96-354, forms 50-129 / 50-281

Herd cash flow & ROI projection

10-year projection with feed, land, labor, vet, fence, capture/haul, commission and mortality. Every input starts blank: no seller "cash flow %" built in. Includes payback year, ROI and a ±20% sensitivity table.

Buy / sell / hold calculator

Compare a ranch offer, auction sale, or holding with your fee, mortality and holding-cost estimates. Flags whether the Section 1231 holding period (24 months cattle/horses, 12 months other livestock) looks met. Your CPA confirms.

Ranch expense ledger (Schedule F)

Log expenses with Schedule F category drop-downs and a capital-vs-operating flag. The summary totals each line and lists capital items to carry into the depreciation schedule.

Hobby-loss checklist + hours log

Rate the nine profit-motive factors of Treas. Reg. §1.183-2(b), check the §183(d) 3-of-5-year presumption, and log hours by tax year (500-hour material-participation test).
