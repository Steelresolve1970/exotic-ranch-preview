Senegal Bushbaby (Northern Lesser Galago)

Galago senegalensis

Overview

Senegal Bushbaby (Northern Lesser Galago) appears on the Exotic Wildlife Association species list as Bushbaby — ESA: Not Listed; CITES: Appendix II. [1]

Native range

Bush babies occupy the forested and bush regions of Africa south of the Sahara. Their range also extends to some nearby islands, including Zanzibar. [2]

Size

0.095–0.3 kg (ADW range). [2]

Lifespan

10 years (captivity, average); 3 to 4 years (wild, typical); 10 years (captivity, typical). [2]

Breeding & gestation

Gestation 110–120 days; offspring per birth: 1–3. [2]

Habitat needs

Lesser bush babies are well-adapted to living in drier areas. They generally occupy the the savannah woodlands south of the Sahara and are excluded only from the southern tip of Africa. ADW biomes: forest, rainforest, scrub forest. [2]

Natural diet

Bush babies are nocturnal and arboreal feeders. Their favorite food is grasshoppers, but they will also consume small birds, eggs, fruits, seeds and flowers. ADW diet type: omnivore. [2]
