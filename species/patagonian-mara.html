Patagonian Mara (Patagonian Cavy)

Dolichotis patagonum

Overview

Patagonian Mara (Patagonian Cavy) appears on the Exotic Wildlife Association species list as Patagonian Cavy — ESA: Not Listed; CITES: Appendix II. [1]

Native range

Dolichotis patagonum is endemic to the open grasslands and shrubland steppes of Argentina, and is distributed between 28°S and 50°S. [2]

Size

Average 8.12 kg (ADW range). [2]

Lifespan

14 years (captivity, average). [2]

Breeding & gestation

Gestation 9111 days; average 100 days; offspring per birth: 1–3; females mature at 8 months. [2]

Habitat needs

Patagonian maras live only in the arid central and southern regions of Argentina. Generally classified as desert, this area exhibits a wide range of distinct microhabitats ranging from sandy plains to thorny shrubland steppes. ADW biomes: desert or dune, savanna or grassland, scrub forest. [2]
