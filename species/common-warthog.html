Common Warthog

Phacochoerus africanus

Overview

Common Warthog appears on the Exotic Wildlife Association species list as Hog (Warthog) — ESA: Not Listed; CITES: N/A. [1]

Native range

Warthogs ( Phacochoerus africanus ) are found outside forested areas in Africa, from Mauritania to Ethiopia and south to Namibia and eastern South Africa. [2]

Size

50–150 kg (ADW range). [2]

Lifespan

15 to 18 years (wild, range); 7 to 11 years (wild, typical); 20.9 years (captivity, average). [2]

Breeding & gestation

Gestation 170–175 days; average 172 days; offspring per birth: 1–7; females mature at 18 to 20 months. [2]

Habitat needs

Common warthogs are found in open and wooded savannas, grass-steppes, and semi-deserts in Africa. ADW biomes: savanna or grassland. Elevation range 0–3000 m. [2]

Natural diet

Common warthogs are primarily grazers but also feed on roots, berries, bark of young trees, and occassionaly carrion. ADW diet type: omnivore. [2]

Handling & capture

Capture myopathy—muscle damage from pursuit, restraint and transport—is often fatal in wild ungulates. Minimize chase time and stress, avoid hot conditions, use appropriate chemical immobilization by a vet, and release/unload quickly. [3]

IUCN Red List: Least Concern. [4]
