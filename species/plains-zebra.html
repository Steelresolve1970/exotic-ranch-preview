Plains Zebra

Equus quagga

Overview

The plains zebra includes several subspecies; Grant's zebra (E. quagga boehmi) is the subspecies commonly kept on Texas ranches (industry usage; not confirmed by a primary source). [1]

Native range

East and southern Africa. [1]

Size

175–385 kg (plains zebra). [1]

Lifespan

~9–20 years wild; 35–40 years captive. [1]

Vet care & vaccination

No vaccines are labeled for this species in the US; any vaccination protocol is extra-label and should be set by a veterinarian experienced with exotic hoofstock. (No specific protocol was verified for this page.)

Handling & capture

Capture myopathy—muscle damage from pursuit, restraint and transport—is often fatal in wild ungulates. Minimize chase time and stress, avoid hot conditions, use appropriate chemical immobilization by a vet, and release/unload quickly. [5]

Conservation status

IUCN Red List: Near Threatened. [6]

General information, not legal advice. USDA APHIS licensing under the Animal Welfare Act applies to dealers and exhibitors; check USDA and your state wildlife and animal-health agencies before buying, moving or selling. [7][8]
